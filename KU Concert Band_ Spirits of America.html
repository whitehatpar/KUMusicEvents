The Kutztown University Bands

The Kutztown University Bands is an all-encompassing band program designed to provide rewarding musical and educational experiences for a collegiate student, whether they are pursuing a degree in Music or otherwise. The Kutztown University Concert Band is comprised of woodwind, brass, and percussion students at the university who demonstrate a commitment to a high level of musicianship. The Concert Band performs multiple concerts in the fall semester on campus.

The ensemble performs the finest wind literature, including “classics” of the repertoire, transcriptions, and new works. The ensemble has joined commissioning consortia for works by Donald Grantham, Richard Daniel Pour, Jonathan Newman, Rob Smith, Bob Mintzer, Philip Glass, and Joan Tower among others and premiered “Obsidian, Concertino for Solo Bb Clarinet and Wind Ensemble” by local composer Kornel Thomas. Additionally, guest artists from music programs like the University of Illinois, Iowa State University, the University of Massachusetts-Amherst, and the University of Akron have worked with our students to continue their development as young musicians who go on to professional playing and teaching careers, or pursuing advanced degrees at some of the best known schools of music in America.

The Concert Band rehearses in the fall semester on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 3–4:15 PM and performs two concerts. The KU Wind Ensemble rehearses in the spring semester on M, W, F from 4:15–5:30 PM and performs two concerts a semester as well. The Symphonic Band meets on T & R from 3–4:20 PM in the spring semester and performs one concert. Membership in all bands is by audition & open to any KU Student. Audition music is available by contacting Dr. Daniel Neuenschwander (neuensch@kutztown.edu) or Dr. Timothy Butler (tbutler@kutztown.edu). Auditions registrations are available through the KU Music Office (music@kutztown.edu or 610-683-4550), Old Main 114, or by contacting Dr. Neuenschwander at the start of each semester.

Students who are interested in pursuing a major or minor in Music should contact the KU Department of Music at 610-683-4550 or via email at music@kutztown.edu. You may also reach out to individual faculty members (see list below) or to Dr. Todd Walker, the department’s Outreach and Audition Coordinator. It is a GOLDEN time to be involved with the music ensembles at KU and the KU Department of Music.

Department of Music: KU Bands Fund

Each year, the KU Department of Music awards music scholarships to deserving and talented students to study music at Kutztown University. In addition, the KU Bands supports students through the funding of attending regional and national conferences, supplying instruments to use in KU performances, and providing collaborations with guest artists.

Band Directors

Dr. Daniel Neuenschwander is in his 18th year as Assistant Professor of Music at Kutztown University where he currently serves as the Director of Bands, Professor of Studio Trombone, and teaches classes in Music Education. He is active as a trombonist in the area, playing both Bass and Tenor Trombone with the esteemed Allentown Band as well as playing in brass chamber groups. Additionally, he has conducted honor bands, done clinics, and adjudicated bands in PA, OH, VA, MD, NJ, IL, and WI. Dan is a member of PMEA, NAfME, PCBDA, and CMS.

Prior to his time at KU, Dan was a faculty member with the University of Illinois Bands, the birthplace of America’s Collegiate Band Programs. In that capacity, he served as the Assistant Director of the Marching Illini (marching band) and directed the U of Illinois Concert Band, the fourth of six concert bands at the U of I. As a public school music educator, Dan was the band director at Canfield High School in Canfield, OH. Under his guidance, the Symphonic Band performed at several referred conferences, including the OMEA State Music Conference held in Columbus, OH in 2001 and the Ohio Band Directors Conference hosted on the campus of the University of Akron in 2002.

Tim Butler is the Assistant Director of the Kutztown University Bands. He serves as the Director of the KU Marching Unit, and teaches courses related to Music Education and Composition. As the department’s Recruitment Coordinator, Dr. Butler is the primary contact for prospective students interested in earning a music degree at Kutztown.

Dr. Butler recently received his Ph.D. in Music Education from the Pennsylvania State University, where he worked primarily with the Penn State Blue Band and Pride of the Lions Pep Band. His dissertation and ongoing research examines students’ leadership development through music, as well as student motivation and music teacher education.

Dr. Butler earned a Bachelor’s degree in Music Education from The Ohio State University and a Master’s degree in Music Education from Bowling Green State University. In the interim, he taught grade 5–12 band at Holgate Local Schools in northwest Ohio.
